Three snapshots taken 2026-10-08, summarised here with charts. The numbers come from VendBuddy's own research; every section links to the full report and to a CSV you can reuse with credit.

Vending Location Index

An aggregate of 36,069 operating US businesses that VendBuddy users looked up as possible vending locations between 2026-04-30 and 2026-10-08, grouped by Google category, state, rating, opening hours and review count. Rows under 5 listings are suppressed. Full write-up: Where Vending Locations Are: 2026 Location Index.

Public vending contract commission rates

We read 28 public vending solicitations, agreements and awards from cities, counties, school districts and colleges (2015 to 2026) and recorded what each states: commission, term, insurance, healthy-vending rules, card readers and service frequency. Private placement agreements are confidential, so this is public-sector only. Full write-up: Vending Commission Rates and Contract Terms 2026.

State of vending operators, Q4 2026

What 628 operator accounts on VendBuddy did in the 90 days to 2026-10-08: which location types and states they searched, and where they are based. It describes VendBuddy's users, not the whole industry. Full write-up: State of Vending Operators, Q4 2026.

Methodology in brief

  • Location Index: operating businesses returned by Lead Finder searches, grouped by Google category. Aggregates only, with small cells suppressed.
  • Contracts: every row links to a public original; "not stated" means the document is silent, and no gaps were filled from other sources.
  • Operators: aggregate counts, a minimum of 20 accounts behind any published figure, test and staff accounts excluded.

Complete methodology lives in each linked report and in the vending-data README.
