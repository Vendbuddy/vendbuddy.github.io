Vending location scoring checklist

Score a possible location on ten points, 0 to 2 each. The total tells you whether to pitch it, negotiate harder, or walk away. Fill it in on screen or print it and take it on site.

vendbuddy.github.io/checklist/ (VendBuddy Open Resources)

1. Foot traffic

How many different people are on site on a normal day?

2. Dwell time

Do people wait or stay long enough to want something?

3. Nearby alternatives

How far is the nearest place to buy food or a drink?

4. Hours of activity

When is the place busy?

5. Decision-maker

Can you reach the person who approves placements?

6. Space and power

Is there a spot with an outlet, clear floor and door clearance?

7. Restocking access

Can you get product in during open hours without a fight?

8. Visibility and security

Will people see the machine, and will it be safe?

9. Existing machines

Is there already a machine or an exclusive contract?

10. Payments and signal

Will a card reader get a connection, and are people likely to pay by card?

Score: 0 / 20

Reading your score

ScoreWhat to do
16 to 20Strong candidate. Book the conversation and prepare a clear proposal.
11 to 15Workable. Fix the low scores first (space, access, payments) or negotiate terms that reflect them.
0 to 10Probably not worth the machine or the time. Revisit only if something changes.

A zero on foot traffic or on decision-maker access usually matters more than the total, so treat either as a stop sign until you have an answer. The scale is a judgement aid, not a prediction of sales.
